M. C. Esher

Maurits Cornelis Escher was a Dutch graphic artist. He was known for his mathematically inspired woodcuts, lithographs, and mezzotints. His work features impossible constructions, explorations of infinity, architecture, and tessellations. I find inspiration in his work, from it's intricate details and mathematical precision.


Joyce Weisbecker

Joyce Weisbecker was the first female video game designer and programmer. She is regarded as the first indie developer, which I find very inspiring.
